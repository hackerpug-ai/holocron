## Executive Summary

The product constitution is still sound: scores move only through cited admitted evidence, ASSAY≠CHALLENGE is checked on resolved identity, and the human holds the done-bit. What v3.0.0 does *not* do is make that constitution implementable on the platform that actually exists. The ADRs say Mastra + Postgres + fleet client; the contracts an implementer would consume (TR 02–05/08, spike-gate oracles, LED/GATE files) still specify a Convex-cloud + SQLite-sidecar + Cohere world, while live mk6 code already shipped a *different* evidence graph, a *different* stage graph, and a `fulcrum` alias of `evidence-research`. Six independent reviewers all returned **NEEDS_FIXES**. The v3.0.0 fleet rewrite of LIS is the strongest part of the PRD; it is sitting on a CONSTITUTION layer that still tells sprint planners to build the deleted sidecar.
