## Executive Summary

The template-collapse architecture itself is genuinely delivered: one `evidence-research` template serves research/deepResearch/subscriptions-research/fulcrum, one parameterized `business-report` template serves all 4 report kinds, subscriptions really invokes `subworkflow:evidence-research` and persists a real Postgres document row, the per-domain platform shells are really gone, and the pipes-4 RED evidence is genuine (archived FAIL output at `.spec/reviews/sprint-22/pipes-4-red-evidence.md`, RED commit `678c89a8`). **However**, the sprint's two headline capability claims do not hold under adversarial inspection: (1) every pipeline "gather" stage is **deterministic scaffolding** — whatsNew headlines/URLs are hash-fabricated, shop products are a hardcoded catalog, business-report TAM/SAM/SOM are hash-derived — and CAP-EMB-01 retrieval is declared "consumed" but never wired; and (2) Human Testing Gate step 6 documents `holo infer:trace <id>`, a command that **does not exist anywhere in the repo** — the gate "passed" step 6 by substituting a different command. Additionally, all six CLI entry points embed `Date.now()` in their default idempotency keys, silently defeating the runtime's (correctly implemented) idempotency/dedup infrastructure.
