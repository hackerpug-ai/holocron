## Executive Summary

The 7-commit delta from the prior clean baseline (`ca014797`) completes the honesty remediation of the share-URL gate path (Blocker B). It does three things, all in the right direction:

1. **Removes the HIGH-4 clipboard theatre** that existed at `ca014797`. At `99758c6b` (`app/document/[id].tsx:478-486`) the handler only does `setLastShareUrl(shareUrl)` → `Share.share({url, message})`.
