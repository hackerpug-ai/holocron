# A section link into a withdrawn document reveals no section

Take a working section anchor for a document, then unshare that document. Open the anchored URL again after the stated bound. The reader must get the withdrawn page carrying its designed copy. The heading text must not appear anywhere in the response, the fragment must not be echoed into visible copy, and no partial content may be served on the theory that a fragment request is somehow narrower than a document request.
