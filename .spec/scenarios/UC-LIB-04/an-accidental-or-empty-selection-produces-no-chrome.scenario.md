# An accidental or empty selection produces no chrome

Perform a zero-width click-drag inside a paragraph, a double-click that lands on whitespace between words, and a selection consisting only of a newline. None may summon the control. Then make a real selection of a full sentence, confirm the control appears, and click elsewhere: the control must disappear. The reading column's whole job is calm, and a control that fires on every stray highlight is the failure the design note names explicitly.
