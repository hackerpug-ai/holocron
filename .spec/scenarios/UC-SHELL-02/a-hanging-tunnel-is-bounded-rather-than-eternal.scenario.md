# A hanging tunnel is bounded rather than eternal

Point the application at a tunnel endpoint that accepts the connection and then never responds. Open Library and time the interface. Within a bounded window of at most fifteen seconds the surface must resolve to the named unreachable state with its copy and a retry control. A skeleton or spinner still animating after thirty seconds fails this scenario, as does a retry press that stacks a second hanging request without cancelling the first.
