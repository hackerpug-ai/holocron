# A document with no figures and no citations has no empty affordances

Open a plain 800-word document with no images, no citations and no headings in the reading column. The column must render all 800 words of clean prose only: no empty figure slots, no sources section with nothing in it, no outline rail, and no anchor controls hovering over paragraphs. The measure must be identical to the measure used by a document that does have figures.
