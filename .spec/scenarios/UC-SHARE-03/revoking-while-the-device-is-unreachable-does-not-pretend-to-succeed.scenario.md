# Revoking while the device is unreachable does not pretend to succeed

Stop the device platform and press unshare. The row must not move to unshared and the interface must not state a propagation bound for a revocation that never happened. The failure must be named in real copy. Confirm the document is still public in Postgres and still resolving publicly afterwards, then restart the platform and revoke for real. A revocation the operator believes happened but which never reached the archive is the worst possible outcome in this whole use case.
